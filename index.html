Before you go

Check official sites before booking
VIETNAM

Get the e-visa

US passports need one. Apply only on the official government site about a month ahead; it takes 3–5 working days.

thithucdientu.gov.vn ↗
THAILAND

Arrival card, no visa

US passports enter visa-free for short stays. Fill in the free Digital Arrival Card within 72 hours before landing on Jun 6.

tdac.immigration.go.th ↗
WEATHER

Hot, with afternoon storms

June is early wet season everywhere we go. Pack light clothes, a rain shell, and plan beach and boat days flexibly in Phuket.

ON THE GROUND

Grab, eSIM, some cash

Grab works in all four cities. A regional eSIM covers both countries. Carry some dong and baht for street food.
